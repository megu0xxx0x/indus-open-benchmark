# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes rigorous disagreement about evidence and methods. Participants must:

- distinguish criticism of a claim from criticism of a person or community;
- disclose conflicts, prior hypotheses, and source access restrictions;
- credit source-country scholars, institutions, excavators, cataloguers, and annotators;
- avoid nationalist, ethnic, linguistic, or religious certainty not supported by evidence;
- document negative results and corrections;
- not harass contributors or pressure custodians to release restricted heritage material.

Maintainers may remove contributions that violate research ethics, rights, privacy, or participant
safety, while preserving a transparent decision record where lawful.
