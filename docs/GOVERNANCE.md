# Governance Seed

An authoritative corpus cannot be governed by one decipherment camp.

## Required separation of roles

- **Corpus team:** images, identifiers, provenance, transcription, uncertainty, and releases.
- **Hypothesis teams:** phonetic, linguistic, administrative, emblematic, and mixed models.
- **Evaluation custodian:** remains organizationally separate from hypothesis
  authors; authenticates and receipts exact `B`/`S` values, retains access
  logs, keeps hidden mappings private, and executes frozen submissions in the
  isolated runtime.
- **Rights and heritage review:** verifies permissions, attribution, and restrictions.
- **Independent red team:** searches for leakage, circular labels, flexible mappings, and omitted
  counterexamples.

Corpus decisions must not be changed to make one hypothesis score better. Hypothesis authors may
submit correction requests, but a separate, documented review decides them.

## Representation

The mature consortium should include institutions and scholars from India and Pakistan, relevant
Gulf and Mesopotamian collections, archaeologists, conservators, epigraphers, historical linguists,
statistics and machine-learning researchers, museum registrars, and data-rights specialists.

Minutes, conflicts, schema changes, releases, retractions, and benchmark rules should be public
except where security, conservation, or embargoed excavation information requires restriction.
