# NMFA global target-source audit — 2026-08-02

**Assessment date:** 2026-08-02 JST

**Scope:** worldwide public primary sources that might join an Indus
inscription to an independently observed direct count, mass, or capacity

**Method:** read-only public-web review; no institution contact, protected
access, bulk acquisition, image corpus, or experiment execution

## Outcome first

No current source or source join was verified to satisfy the complete NMFA
minimum: a rights-compatible, finite population with complete inscription
observations `X`, reviewed original physical units `F`, transitive dependence
groups `G`, canonical context `C`, value-free eligibility `E`, and a direct
non-inscription-derived target `Y` for at least 160 eligible `G`.

This is a verified-evidence result, not a claim that suitable unpublished data
cannot exist. The number currently demonstrated to satisfy every gate is below
160. Several sources are useful building blocks, but the missing join is
between complete inscriptions and individual direct measurements under usable
rights and complete dependence/context evidence.

The most important corrections from this audit are:

- ICIT is the strongest sign-side corpus candidate, but it is managed-access;
  no open bulk licence or public API was verified.
- NMMA's Lothal museum page lists exactly 133 records in the **Seals &
  Sealing** category. That category alone therefore has an eligible ceiling of
  133 before any `E/F/G` exclusion and cannot satisfy the 160-`G` gate. The
  figure is not a count of every potentially inscribed object in every Lothal
  category.
- Ascalone supplies a strong mass/metrology catalogue, not an established
  sign-to-mass join. Its chapter's 29 inscribed weights are principally
  Mesopotamian/Elamite objects, not 29 Indus-script anchors.
- A source count, an eligible-object count, and a same-object `X/Y` count are
  different quantities. No audited source demonstrates the last of these at
  NMFA scale.

The audit inspected field availability and a small number of public examples.
Any example whose measurement or outcome state was viewed is exposed
development evidence. It cannot later be relabelled as sealed holdout or
prospective evidence.

## Dated catalogue facts and count discipline

- Two official 2023 ICIT snapshots differ slightly. The IIT Gandhinagar
  abstract reports the figures above, while a Government of India
  [Ministry of Culture working-group report](https://www.culture.gov.in/files/reports_documents/WorkingGroupReport.pdf)
  reports, as of May 2023, 4,674 objects, 5,659 texts, 19,869 sign occurrences,
  and 713 signs. These are dated snapshots, not interchangeable live totals.
- Official Government of India releases reported that NMMA had documented
  1.720 million antiquities by [23 March 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2243793&lang=1&reg=3)
  and that approximately 1.248 million had been uploaded by
  [2 April 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2248340&lang=2&reg=48).
  Neither national total estimates an eligible Indus sign/direct-target join.
- The Penn bulk page identified its downloadable snapshot as last updated
  **2026-07-22**. On 2026-08-02, the official
  [search interface](https://collections.penn.museum/collections/search.php)
  displayed 430,810 objects, 521 results in the Harappan facet, and 1,234 in
  the weight facet. The latter two are separate filters, not a 521- or
  1,234-object intersection.
- The Science Museum Group's dated
  [2025-04-09 bulk snapshot](https://coimages.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/datasets/index.html)
  contains 525,595 object records. Its simplified CSV field list has no
  explicit mass or dimensions column; richer nested JSON still requires
  record-level review. The current [online-collection description](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/about)
  says that more than 500,000 objects and 50,000 archival documents are
  online. Older catalogue totals must not be presented as current.

All live catalogue totals should retain an access date. A portal-wide count,
a topical facet, an eligible-object population, and an object-level `X/Y`
intersection must never be substituted for one another.

## Why apparent shortcuts fail

- A catalogue field named `weight` is not evidence that a complete positive
  mass was measured for each inscribed original.
- A blank, zero, fragmentary, estimated, or unit-ambiguous mass must not be
  promoted to a valid direct target merely because the field exists.
- A vessel dimension or geometric volume is a proxy, not direct capacity under
  the current protocol.
- A collection of archaeological weights is not an inscription/measurement
  join merely because the objects come from an Indus site.
- `Indus` or `Harappan` is a broad cultural label, `seal` does not guarantee a
  complete legible sequence, and `weight` does not imply that the object is an
  inscribed seal whose own mass supplies `Y`.
- A sealing count is not 100 independent observations if several impressions
  derive from one seal, bundle, workshop, or excavation assemblage.
- An API, download button, or viewable image does not establish retention,
  derivation, aggregate publication, custodial transfer, or prize-submission
  rights.
- Thousands of inscription sequences do not solve the problem when the direct
  independent target is absent.

## Tamil Nadu one-million-dollar announcement

The announcement itself is authentic. Tamil Nadu's Directorate of Information
and Public Relations published
[Press Release No. 37 dated 5 January 2025](https://dipr.tn.gov.in/ords/r/dipr/info-prdept103/press-release1?cs=17R0H-bKZMbOX4AlR_1awqes0TjNMjZfSiguFGs-EZAuLpJ7oTo7mfnIeUQKJYwuwGZWO8x9ra6Rt5S1dG70WFw&p33_file_id=11209&request=APPLICATION_PROCESS%3DGET_FILE),
recording Chief Minister M. K. Stalin's announcement of US$1 million for an
individual or organization that finds a way or method that helps clearly
understand the Indus culture's writing system. That cautious paraphrase is
broader than “the first person to completely decipher the script.” The
official amount is in US dollars; any yen equivalent needs its own exchange
rate and date.

M. K. Stalin is the Chief Minister of the Indian state of Tamil Nadu, not the
Prime Minister of India. The official [Indus100 programme](https://indus100.com/speakers/)
dates the centenary conference to 5–7 January 2025 and identifies the Chief
Minister as its inaugurator. The conference's official
[paper-presentation page](https://indus100.com/paper-presentation/) and the
[RMRL Indus Research Centre](https://www.rmrl.in/en/irc) continue to describe
the script as undeciphered.

As of 2026-08-02, this audit did not locate on the reviewed Tamil Nadu
government, Indus100, or RMRL official sites a separate government order or
Gazette notice, application rules, deadline, submission endpoint, judging
body, evaluation/validation criteria, payment conditions, intellectual-
property terms, or confirmation of an operating application process. This is
a search result, not proof that no unpublished or later-issued procedure
exists. The accurate status is:

> The Tamil Nadu government announcement is real; an actionable and presently
> claimable award procedure has not yet been verified.

The announcement does not call the US$1 million award the “Iravatham
Mahadevan Prize.” In the same release, the award and the separate funding for
an Iravatham Mahadevan research chair are different measures.

No submission or contact was attempted.

## Highest-information next route

The shortest empirical route is not another unrestricted corpus scrape. It is
a bounded, value-blind source-frame audit:

1. use ICIT only as the sign-side index/join candidate under verified access
   and rights;
2. enumerate NMMA/Lothal and Penn candidates from non-target fields, while
   preserving the 133-record Lothal-category ceiling and all missing/invalid
   measurement states;
3. use Ascalone and HARP as mass/metrology and context references rather than
   assuming that their weights carry Indus inscriptions;
4. close originals, duplicates, common seals/impressions, assemblages, and
   excavation dependencies into `F/G`; and
5. stop before target inspection if the complete rights-compatible prevalue
   frame has fewer than 160 candidate `G` even under the most favorable
   remaining Y/status outcomes. This is only an upper-bound screen; actual
   base E and the `>=160` eligible-G gate are decided post-seal without target
   release to the evaluator.

That question will require the source-free gate implementation plus separate
signed deterministic `PREMETADATA_READY` and `PREVALUE_READY` evaluators and
receipts. Those preflight evaluators are not implemented, so the current
artifact authorizes no claim-instance metadata, protected X, or protected Y
access. Only after they exist and are externally verified may authorized,
role-separated custodians curate and seal X and Y; the post-seal evaluator
itself remains value-blind. Institution queries or permission requests require
separate explicit authority; this audit neither sends them nor treats public
availability as permission.
