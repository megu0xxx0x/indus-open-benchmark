## Outcome first

This draft fixes a narrow falsification design for one question: can one small,
positive additive count of predeclared token classes predict exactly one
independently curated `count`, `mass`, or `capacity` target across unseen
domains?

It contains no real source ID, artifact ID, sign, transcription, target value,
realized split assignment, instantiated prediction, result, execution, or
source access. Its hypothesis-schema
status is `draft`, every registration field is null/false, every observation
reference is empty, and its sole prospective claim has confidence zero and
exactly three kill criteria.
It is not a preregistration receipt, functional result, sign reading, language
assignment, translation, decipherment, submission, or prize claim.

## Why this slice

The project efficiency audit ranks context-rich functional anchors above more
generic verifier infrastructure because a surviving quantity relation could
eliminate broad hypothesis classes. A failure is also informative: the model
must lose under locked domain holdouts, matched controls, and later prospective
data rather than being rescued by changing direction, allographs, units, or
subsets after inspection.

The draft uses the existing `hypothesis.schema.json`; it adds no schema, CLI,
runner, dependency, source access, or result surface. The schema has no typed
metric, split, or null fields, so the exact protocol is carried by closed rule
IDs and formalism strings. Decision-critical fragments and the complete
payload digest are guarded by focused tests. A future executable version needs
a separately registered typed evaluator contract.

## Data roles and eligibility

| Role | Future content | Isolation requirement |
|---|---|---|
| `X` | approved complete transcription | holder receives no `Y` or sealed mapping |
| `Y` | one direct target family and one canonical unit | curator receives no `X` or prediction |
| `E` | value-free Y eligibility/unit/completeness commitment | one closed row per physical unit `F`; no numeric target |
| `C` | four archaeological axes plus frozen non-outcome documentation/measurement nuisance fields | four axes drive the split; the full tuple drives nulls |
| `F` | one physical-original all-side analysis unit | object target counted once, never once per side |
| `G` | transitive leakage/dependence superfamily of `F` | one split/resampling/primary-count unit |
| `H` | sealed domain cells and mapping | unsealed once after model freeze |
| `N` | specificity control and domain-matched null | fixed before `H` is opened |

The independent curators first freeze the exact source/data roles. The Y
curator receipts `E` over the complete population without exposing a numeric
target. A controlled evaluator may join development/validation `X/Y` only
after source, eligibility, superfamily, context, and split state are frozen;
the holdout mapping and numeric values remain hidden until the model is
frozen. Operational role separation limits leakage; it does not prove that
two people, AI systems, or organizations are independent.

Each future unit must be a reviewed original, not a replica or modern object,
with complete unambiguous transcription, exact physical identity, all four
context axes, rights, and provenance. `Y` must be a direct non-inscription
measurement: a nonnegative integer count or a finite positive mass/capacity in
one locked unit. Values inferred from an inscription, title, description,
claimed meaning, or dimension proxy are forbidden. Missing or conflicting
required data are excluded before splitting; imputation and result-dependent
selection are forbidden. `E` carries only source/digest bindings, the locked
target family/unit, an eligibility boolean, and one closed reason code for
every population unit. Score-zero observations remain eligible. Every context
axis uses one frozen mutually exclusive vocabulary and alias/parent-child
crosswalk; ambiguous, multiple, overlapping, or incompatible values are
ineligible before the split.

Before splitting, freeze one provenance policy. Either all `X/Y` must use one
prespecified transcription/annotation and measurement
source/method/calibration regime, or `C` must also contain complete canonical
nuisance fields for transcription/publication source and edition/batch, plus
measurement source, method, campaign, instrument/calibration, and batch.
Explicit `not_applicable` values require a frozen justification; unknown
nuisance provenance is ineligible. This prevents documentation or measurement
batch effects from masquerading as a symbol/quantity relation.

The current Penn-derived context-anchor entries are metadata-only and retain
false originality, transcription, meaning, and field-number approvals. They
therefore cannot instantiate this primary test. Replica/modern rows remain
negative controls only.

## Frozen model class

The future registered candidate is exactly one positive additive score:

`S(x) = sum_j(w_j * n_j(x))`

For a selected `F`, `x` is every eligible side/inscription of that physical
original in one frozen canonical side/line/token order. It is never a
target-selected side. Freeze `L_total` as its total eligible-token count and
`L_distinct` as its distinct frozen token/allograph-class count; both are
generic length/complexity controls.

- one to eight pairwise-disjoint token/allograph classes;
- integer weights from 1 through 16 with greatest common divisor one;
- one direction/segmentation/damage/allograph policy selected using only
  development and validation, then frozen;
- no site, period, medium, object, artifact, or inscription coefficient;
- no interaction, negative weight, per-row exception, direction flip,
  post-unseal allograph merge, or post-unseal refit.

`S` is an ordinal rank prediction only. The canonical-unit conversion
normalizes and ranks `Y`; the protocol fits no numeric `S`-to-`Y` reading.
This deliberately small functional model does not encode a word, sound,
language, commodity, office, person, or full administrative reading.

## Leakage-safe strict domain split

First build `F` by collapsing every side/inscription, catalog alias,
publication view/image hash, cross-edition record, confirmed fragment of the
same original, and exact duplicate record of one physical original. A distinct
cast, replica, impression, or mold product is never merged into `F`. Then
close `G` across every `F`, eligible or not, when connected by a
cast/impression, common mold/template, exact normalized sequence, or a known
common production batch, workshop, recovery locus, or assemblage. This makes
all known non-independence one split/bootstrap/permutation unit and prevents
one object-level target from being counted once per side. A declared
possible/unresolved relation must be
conservatively unioned or make every involved unit ineligible; unknown is
never treated as evidence of independence. Conflicting superfamilies are
ineligible.

Let `M_G` be the `E=true` members of `G`. A `G` is eligible only when `M_G` is
nonempty and every member has complete canonical context. After cells are
fixed, select one primary `F` per `G` without `Y` or model scores. In a
holdout cell, hash-select among `M_G` whose canonical context triggered that
cell; in the complement, select among all of `M_G`. Retain the rest only for
secondary consistency reporting. After opening `H`, a correction or new edge
among historical `F`, or an incoming `F` that bridges at least two historical
`G` or crosses a historical partition, is `BENCHMARK_INVALID` and requires a
fresh version and holdout. An incoming `F` connected to exactly one historical
`G` is absorbed into it and excluded from the prospective cohort without
changing the historical score. Relations only among incoming `F` close within
the prospective cohort.

At least 160 eligible independent `G` are required. For each axis, define the
context set of a `G` from every member `F`, including `E=false` members, using
the frozen canonical alias/descendant closure. Enumerate domain tuples
`(site, period, medium, object_type)` and construct disjoint cells in this
order:

1. `H_site`: every `G` whose site set intersects the selected site closure;
2. `H_period`: among the remainder, every `G` whose period set intersects the
   selected period closure;
3. `H_medium`: among the remainder, every `G` whose medium set intersects the
   selected medium closure;
4. `H_object`: among the remainder, every `G` whose object-type set intersects
   the selected object closure.

Each cell must contain at least 20 `G`; every assigned `G` must have an
`E=true` member that triggered that cell; their sealed union `H` and the
complement must each contain at least 80. With numeric `Y` sealed, the
custodian applies the complete `E`, builds `F`, closes `G` from frozen `X/C`
and source-bound
relation evidence, canonicalizes `C`, and freezes the eligible-inventory
digest. Only then does an externally verifiable unpredictable source provide
one nonce; nobody may choose, retry, or resalt it. The nonce, inventory digest,
`E/F/G/M_G/C`, and minimum SHA-256 select one eligible tuple. The selected canonical
groups, including aliases and descendants, are absent from the complement.
Its `G`-hash order repeats `[development, development, validation]`, yielding
at least 54 development and 26 validation superfamilies at the minimum size.
Joined development/validation data are released after this freeze; `H` is
opened once after exactly one score is frozen.

This strict cell design replaces the earlier provisional 50/25/25 random-split
idea; the two designs must not be combined.

## Passing gates

| Gate | Exact minimum |
|---|---:|
| Sealed overall average-rank Spearman `rho_H` | `>= 0.40` |
| `delta_length = rho_H - max(0, rho(L_total,Y), rho(L_distinct,Y))` | `>= 0.10` |
| 10,000 cell-stratified `G`-bootstrap `lower(CI95_rho)` | `> 0.20` |
| Same paired bootstrap `lower(CI95_delta_length)` | `> 0.00` |
| Spearman rho in each of the four sealed cells | `>= 0.20` |
| Distinct levels in both score and target | `>= 4` |
| New prospective independent superfamilies | `>= 20` |
| Prospective Spearman rho | `>= 0.40` |
| Prospective `delta_length` | `>= 0.10` |
| Prospective paired-bootstrap `lower(CI95_rho)` | `> 0.20` |
| Prospective paired-bootstrap `lower(CI95_delta_length)` | `> 0.00` |
| Prospective 99,999-permutation add-one p | `<= 0.01` |
| Valid prospective outputs | `100%` |

All gates are conjunctive. Each bootstrap resamples the same `G` once and
recomputes both the candidate and both length correlations, so the difference
interval is paired. Undefined bootstrap candidate rho is `-1`; an undefined
length correlation is `0`; no draw is discarded or redrawn. The point
`delta_length` is a prespecified effect-size margin, while its paired interval
must independently exclude zero. Deterministic randomness is derived from the
later externally bound frozen-protocol digest with separate operation labels.

## Matched controls and single-claim boundary

Each control uses 99,999 deterministic runs keyed by the frozen protocol
digest, a distinct label, and run index; repeated draws are retained.

- `N1` is a token-specificity negative control, not a global no-association
  test. Before `H` opens, freeze every selected class's pool of disjoint
  noncandidate classes outside all selected allograph groups and in the same
  development occurrence-count and `G`-prevalence power-of-two bins. Each run draws an injective mapping,
  preserves frozen weights, and refits nothing. Every class needs at least two
  alternatives; inability to construct a mapping makes the version ineligible.
- `N2` is the no-association null. Within each assigned holdout cell, freeze
  every primary `F`'s exact full-`C` tuple: canonical
  `(site, period, medium, object_type)` plus all documentation/measurement
  nuisance fields required by the frozen provenance policy. At least 80% and
  at least 64 holdout `G` must be in full-`C` strata of size two or more before
  target reveal; otherwise the version is ineligible. Permute whole-`G`
  targets only within those strata and leave singleton strata fixed,
  preserving every observed context and nuisance association.

Undefined/nonfinite control correlations become `+1`, never discarded or
redrawn. Sort the 99,999 correlations ascending; `q0.99` is the 99,000th
one-based value. For both controls, observed `rho_H` must strictly exceed
`q0.99`. `N1` uses the descriptive
`specificity_tail_fraction = (1 + count(rho_N1 >= rho_H)) / 100000 <= 0.01`;
because its assignments are not an exchangeable global null, it is not called
an inferential p value. `N2` uses the one-sided randomization
`p = (1 + count(rho_N2 >= rho_H)) / 100000 <= 0.01`. Both must pass.

Exactly one target, unit, ordinal score, direction, metric, and primary claim
may use `H` (`K=1`). Any second confirmatory primary requires a separately
registered protocol and fresh `H`; a post-access alternative is exploratory
only and cannot rescue failure.

## Prospective replication and terminal handling

A future registered version commits the source frame, complete population and
eligibility rule, acquisition start, close date, canonical source ordering,
ordinal model, target-unit conversion, policies, and prediction algorithm
before `H` opens. It also commits a design-stage sensitivity/power rationale
for the fixed frame and close date. The minimum 20 new `G` is only an
evaluability floor, not evidence that the design is adequately powered.
Every prospective unit's first eligibility-qualifying source-bound
availability, and the first availability of any associated `X/C/F/G/Y`
information, must be strictly later than the immutable model, policy, and
prediction-algorithm freeze; those timestamps require receipts. A unit or any
of its information that was available earlier is historical and ineligible
for the prospective cohort.
At the close date it includes every qualifying unit rather than stopping at a
favorable count. With prospective targets sealed, rebuild `F`, apply `E`, and
re-close relations across every historical and incoming `F`, including
`E=false` members. An incoming `F` joined to exactly one historical `G` is
absorbed and excluded from the prospective count. Bridging two or more
historical `G`, or any historical partition, is `BENCHMARK_INVALID` and
requires fresh `H`. Relations only among incoming `F` close into one
prospective `G`.

For each new `G`, require nonempty `M_G` and complete `C`, select one member of
`M_G` by the frozen target-blind hash, compute one integer ordinal `S` plus
`L_total/L_distinct`, and commit the complete `G`-to-`S` manifest. While `Y`
remains sealed, require at least 20 new `G`, one valid `S` for 100% of them,
and at least four distinct `S` levels. Freeze the full-`C` tuple, including
the nuisance fields, for every primary `F`; at least 80% and at least 16
prospective `G` must lie in strata of size two or more. Do not reveal `Y` if
any pre-reveal condition fails. Only after all pass may targets be revealed
once; numeric `S`-to-`Y` fitting and refitting are forbidden.

Fewer than 20 eligible `G` at the deadline is
`INSUFFICIENT_PROSPECTIVE_DATA`: it is neither a pass nor scientific
refutation. Fewer than four prospective score levels before reveal, or target
levels after reveal, is `INSUFFICIENT_PROSPECTIVE_VARIATION` with the same
non-pass/non-refutation meaning. An evaluable tranche must also pass
`rho >= 0.40`, `delta_length >= 0.10`, paired-bootstrap
`lower(CI95_rho) > 0.20` and `lower(CI95_delta_length) > 0.00`, and a
99,999-run one-sided whole-`G` target permutation within frozen full-`C`
strata with add-one `p <= 0.01`. Insufficient
movable context support is `INSUFFICIENT_PROSPECTIVE_CONTEXT_SUPPORT`, also a
non-pass/non-refutation. Benchmark defects produce
`BENCHMARK_INVALID` and no score.
The two prospective random operations use distinct labels
`prospective-bootstrap-v1` and `prospective-null-n2-v1`. A future typed
evaluator must freeze their exact seed encoding and generator semantics before
`H` opens.
Candidate missing/extra/duplicate/nonfinite outputs, crashes, timeouts, or
resource exhaustion produce `CANDIDATE_INVALID/NO_GO`; partial scoring and
imputation are forbidden. An evaluator fault can be voided and rerun only if
no feedback escaped and the same `H` remained sealed; otherwise fresh holdout
superfamilies are required. Exact terminal precedence is
`BENCHMARK_INVALID > EVALUATION_VOID > CANDIDATE_INVALID/NO_GO >`
`CONFIRMATORY_NO_GO > INSUFFICIENT_PROSPECTIVE_* > PROSPECTIVE_NO_GO >`
`FUNCTIONAL_ANCHOR_CANDIDATE`.

The phases matter: evaluate the sealed holdout first. Any generalization or
matched-control failure is terminal `CONFIRMATORY_NO_GO` and cannot be hidden
by later prospective insufficiency. Only an H-passing candidate advances to
prospective insufficiency, prospective failure, or the final narrow label.

## Exactly three kill criteria

1. `NMFA-KILL-001`: kill or revise if an overall/cell rho is undefined or
   nonfinite, or if the overall rho, either paired-bootstrap lower bound, any
   domain-cell rho, generic-length improvement, or distinct-level gate fails.
2. `NMFA-KILL-002`: kill or revise if the `N1` specificity quantile/tail gate
   or the `N2` no-association quantile/p gate fails.
3. `NMFA-KILL-003`: after a complete evaluable prospective tranche exists,
   kill or revise if rho is below 0.40, length improvement is below 0.10,
   either paired-bootstrap lower gate fails, or the full-context permutation
   p is above 0.01. Missing/invalid `S` is handled earlier as
   `CANDIDATE_INVALID/NO_GO`; insufficient sample size, variation, or context
   support remains a non-pass rather than refutation.

Any post-freeze change to data roles, target, unit, eligibility row, context
or nuisance vocabulary, policy, model, historical `F/G`, split, metric,
threshold, control, prospective frame/cutoff, code, dependencies, or runtime
creates a new version, external receipt, and confirmatory holdout. The only
allowed relation updates under the frozen prospective rule are incoming-only
edges and attachment of an incoming `F` to exactly one historical `G`; neither
may alter historical partition membership or scores.
