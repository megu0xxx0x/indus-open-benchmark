# Tamil Nadu US$1 Million Indus-Script Prize — Status Audit

**Cut-off:** 2026-07-27
**Decision:** the announcement is authentic; an operational submission scheme
is not publicly verified.

## What is established

On 2025-01-05, at the opening of the Discovery of Indus Civilization
Centenary International Conference in Chennai, Tamil Nadu Chief Minister
M. K. Stalin announced a prize of US$1 million. The contemplated recipient is
an individual or organization that presents a way to solve the Indus-script
problem which is accepted by archaeologists as a valid solution.

This was a statement by a **state Chief Minister**, not by the Prime Minister
of India. “Lin Prime Minister” is a mistaken attribution.

The strongest currently accessible primary record is the Government of Tamil
Nadu Information and Public Relations Department's 2026 collection of the
Chief Minister's speeches. The relevant Tamil text appears on PDF page 83. It
states the amount, the individual-or-organization eligibility concept, and the
requirement that archaeologists accept the proposed route to understanding the
script. A Government retrospective also republishes the announcement in
English. DIPR Press Release No. 37 of 2025-01-05 is the contemporaneous
official record, although its generated download URL can time out.

## What is not established

The public official sources checked through the cut-off did not reveal:

- a Government Order, administrative sanction, cabinet instrument, or Gazette
  notice implementing the prize;
- a dedicated appropriation, financial sanction, escrow, or named prize fund;
- an application call, form, portal, postal address, or official contact;
- opening or closing dates;
- detailed eligibility, submission language, format, or evidence rules;
- a named judging panel, conflict-of-interest rules, review procedure, appeal
  route, or reproducibility criteria;
- a designated administering institution;
- a shortlist, winner, payment, withdrawal, cancellation, or replacement
  announcement.

“Not found” is deliberately time- and search-bounded. It does not prove that
no internal or unindexed record exists, and it does not prove cancellation.
It does mean that the announcement must not be represented as a contest to
which a submission can presently be sent.

## Official-source checks

| Channel | Result at cut-off |
|---|---|
| Tamil Nadu DIPR | Original announcement identified; no later public rules, opening, award, or withdrawal notice found |
| Tamil Nadu Department of Archaeology | Current schemes and site search showed other applications, but no US$1 million submission scheme |
| Tamil Nadu budget 2025–26 | Archaeology, an Indus Valley Cultural Gallery, and other culture spending appear; no dedicated US$1 million prize line was identified |
| Demand No. 29, Tourism–Art and Culture | A generic `Prizes and Awards` line exists, but its 2025–26 estimate is ₹2,000 and does not identify this prize |
| Tamil Nadu Gazette / indexed Government Orders | No implementing record found under the English and Tamil search terms used |
| Tamil Nadu Legislature search | No prize record found in the searchable current-assembly material |
| RMRL / Indus Research Centre | The separately announced Iravatham Mahadevan Chair is documented; the site does not identify itself as prize administrator or provide prize rules |
| `indusscript.in` / `tngraffiti.in` | Research and corpus tools, not prize-submission portals |

The budget finding must not be misstated as “the prize budget is ₹2,000.”
Rather, no dedicated appropriation was identified in the reviewed 2025–26
documents. A later or differently classified appropriation remains possible.
The complete 2026–27 budget set could not be reliably retrieved during this
audit, so no negative conclusion is drawn from that year.

## Separate announcements must not be conflated

The same event included a ₹2 crore announcement for an Iravatham Mahadevan
Chair and annual awards for researchers. RMRL now states that the Chair was
established in 2025. That does not establish administration or funding of the
US$1 million prize.

One paper in the conference proceedings mentions a possible second
US$1 million offer. The paper does not identify an implementing institution or
rules, and the proceedings disclaim institutional responsibility for
contributors' claims. It is therefore not counted as a verified second prize.

## Operational rule for this project

The machine-readable state remains:

```text
announcement_authentic = true
operational_submission_scheme_verified = false
submission_attempt_allowed = false
winner_or_payment_verified = false
```

Do not send an application, claim entitlement, or name an administrator.
Any future inquiry requires verified sender authority, a confirmed addressee,
and separate explicit approval.
